Delete your account

You can ask Lanternwise Labs to delete your account and the personal information tied to it at any time, even if you no longer have the app installed.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@lanternwise.app from the email address on your account, with the subject "Account deletion request".
  2. Tell us which app the account is for (for example, Cookie Manager).
  3. We'll confirm by email, and we'll complete the deletion within 30 days.

Cookie Manager: what gets deleted

  • Your account: name, email address, and login
  • Your membership in any troops
  • Customers and orders you entered for your scouts
  • Diagnostic reports you sent

What may be kept

  • Troop records: some records you took part in, such as a signed transfer of cookies or money, belong to the troop's financial records. We keep these for the troop with your name and signature removed or anonymized.
  • Scouts: if you are a guardian, your scout's records stay with the troop for the season unless you also ask us to delete them, or the troop is deleted.
  • Troop cookie managers: to delete an entire troop and all its data, say so in your email. If you're the only manager, we'll confirm before deleting the troop.
  • Billing records: payment records held by Stripe may be kept as long as tax and accounting laws require.
To delete only some of your data, such as a single customer or scout, without closing your account, email us and tell us what you'd like removed.

For more detail, see the Cookie Manager privacy policy.
