Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Lanternwise Labs is an independent developer that builds small apps for families and community groups. This page explains how we handle information in general. Each app also has its own policy with the specifics of what it collects.

App-specific policies

  • Cookie Manager

Our principles

  • We don't sell your information, and we don't share it for advertising.
  • No ads and no third-party advertising trackers in our apps.
  • We collect only what an app needs to work, and say so in that app's policy.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time. See Delete your account.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) for security and reliability. We don't use those logs to identify you.

Children

Our apps are intended for use by adults, such as parents, guardians, and volunteer leaders. Some apps let adults record limited information about children in their care (for example, a scout's name and cookie sales). We don't knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we'll also let users know in the affected app.
