Cookie Manager Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Cookie Manager helps troop volunteers and families track cookie inventory, booth sales, customer orders, and money during cookie season. It is developed by Lanternwise Labs, an independent developer. Cookie Manager is not affiliated with or endorsed by Girl Scouts of the USA or any council.

This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. Our general privacy policy also applies.

Who uses the app

Cookie Manager is designed for adults: troop cookie managers, other troop leaders, and parents or guardians. Information about scouts, who may be minors, is entered and managed by those adults.

Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password. Your password is stored in hashed form and we can't read it. We also store your role in a troop (for example, troop cookie manager or parent/guardian) and which troop or troops you belong to.

Troop and scout information

Troop cookie managers and guardians can enter:

  • Scout names and the parents or guardians linked to each scout, including guardian names and email addresses (for example, from a roster import)
  • Sales goals, cookie inventory, transfers, booth sign-ups, reward selections, and money owed or paid

Customer information

To track orders and deliveries, users can enter information about their cookie customers: name, phone number, email address, delivery address, the cookies ordered, notes, and payment status and method (for example, cash, check number, or a digital payment service). The app does not collect or store credit or debit card numbers for customer purchases.

If you enter information about other people, such as customers or other guardians, please make sure you have their permission to do so.

Signatures and photos

  • Signatures: when cookies or money change hands, the giver and receiver can sign on screen. The signature is saved with that transfer record.
  • Receipt photos: if you choose to, you can take or pick a photo of a council cookie receipt. The app only uses the camera or your photo library when you start that action.

Booth information

Booth locations, addresses, times, shift sign-ups, the scouts and adults working each shift, and booth sales totals.

Calendar

If you choose to, the app can add your booth shifts to your device's calendar. It asks for permission to write calendar events only. The app does not read your existing calendar events or send them to us.

Diagnostic logs

The app keeps a technical log on your device to help troubleshoot problems. Logs are sent to us only if you tap "Send to App Team" on the Diagnostics screen. A report includes the app version, device platform and operating system version, the app's activity log, and any note you add. Logs may contain identifiers, such as record IDs, that relate to your troop's data.

Billing

If a troop purchases a paid plan, payment is handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your payment details directly. We receive the subscription status, plan, and number of seats, but not your full card number.

What we don't collect

  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics SDKs
  • No location tracking (booth addresses are entered by hand)
  • No access to your contacts
  • No selling or renting of personal information

How we use information

  • To run the app's features: accounts, troop rosters, inventory, transfers, orders, booths, rewards, and reports
  • To send receipts and booth notifications by email to the guardians listed for a scout and to the people involved in a transfer
  • To sync data between your devices and other members of your troop, including after working offline
  • To manage troop subscriptions and billing
  • To troubleshoot problems you report to us

Who can see your information

Your troop. Data belongs to a troop and is visible to troop members based on their role. Troop cookie managers can see troop-wide information. Parents and guardians see their own scouts' information plus shared troop information, such as booth schedules. Data from one troop isn't visible to other troops.

Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of users or others.

Where information is stored

Information is stored on our server and in a local database on your device, so the app keeps working without a signal. Data is encrypted in transit between the app and our server (HTTPS). Photos and signatures are stored with access restricted to authorized troop members.

Retention and deletion

  • We keep troop data while the troop's account is active, so troops can reconcile and report on past seasons.
  • To delete your account and the personal information tied to it, follow the steps on our account deletion page. We complete requests within 30 days.
  • Some troop records you took part in, such as a signed transfer, may be kept for the troop's financial records with your personal details removed or anonymized, unless the troop is also deleted.
  • A troop cookie manager can ask us to delete the whole troop and all of its data.
  • Diagnostic reports are deleted after 90 days.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.

Your choices and rights

You can view and update most of your information in the app. You can also contact us to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we'll honor them.

Children's privacy

Cookie Manager is intended for adults. Scouts' information is entered by their parents, guardians, or troop leaders and is limited to what's needed for cookie sales: name, inventory, sales, goals, and rewards. We don't knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. A parent or guardian can contact us at any time to review or delete their child's information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we'll also notify users in the app.
