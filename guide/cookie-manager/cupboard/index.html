Set pickup hours, handle family requests and returns, and manage appointments.

Families who are short on cookies, or who have extras to bring back, ask you through the app. If you turn on cupboard scheduling, they also book a time to come by.

Set the cupboard address and turn on booking

  1. Open Settings & Permissions → Cookie Cupboard.
  2. Enter the Cookie Cupboard Address. Families see it on their home screen with an Open in Maps button.
  3. Turn on Enable Cupboard Schedule Hours if you want families to book times.
  4. Choose how far ahead families must book or cancel (any time, or 2, 4, 12, 24, or 48 hours).
  5. Tap Save.

Post your hours

  1. On the Cookie Cupboard section tap Set Cupboard Hours, or open Cupboard Schedule from the dashboard and tap Set Cupboard Hours. Save your settings first so they apply to the hours you add.
  2. Under Add Hours, pick the days, the Opens and Closes times, and how many weeks to repeat. A preview shows what will be created.
  3. Tap Add Hours.

Each day becomes its own session that families book in 15-minute times, one family per time. The database refuses a double booking and overlapping hours. Each session shows how many times are booked, and Cancel Hours cancels a day (which cancels the bookings inside it).

Times use your phone's time zone.

How family requests work

On each scout's card, families tap Request or Return Cookies. The form has Request and Return tabs. For a request they enter boxes and cases per cookie (it starts with whatever the scout is short on), plus an optional note. A return can't be more than the scout has at home. If booking is on, they pick a time in the same step. One visit can hold several requests and returns, for example a swap or two siblings.

What you do

Open Requests & Returns from the dashboard. Sections: Waiting for a Decision, Approved, and Earlier.

  1. Tap Approve or Reject on a new request.
  2. For an approved one, tap Give Cookies (Cupboard ➔ Scout) or Take Back Cookies (Scout ➔ Cupboard). This opens the transfer form, where you enter what actually changed hands and collect signatures.
  3. Back on the request, tap Mark Fulfilled.

Families can cancel their own request while it's Requested or Approved.

Appointments

Cupboard Schedule lists visits by day with what to hand over and take back. Mark a visit No-Show or Cancel Visit. A visit completes when its requests are closed. If a family misses or cancels a visit, their requests stay open and they see Pick a Time to book again.

Past the cutoff families can't book or cancel on their own and need to contact you. You can always change a booking.
