Rewards are set up on Reward Tiers (dashboard → Season Management). Families see their own progress and choose on their Rewards screen. You track everyone on Scout Reward Status.

How a scout's box total is figured

A scout's total toward rewards is: boxes taken (Responsible For, delivered or not) + booth credits for real cookies + all donation boxes + any adjustment you add (for example, Digital Cookie boxes that were shipped). Tiers are cumulative: a scout earns every tier at or below her total.

Build the tiers

  1. Tap Add Tier. Enter the Box Threshold, a Tier Title, and an optional description.
  2. Two switches: Count donation boxes only (HTH / GOC) for donation-only tiers, and Late-season bonus goal? to highlight a tier for families.
  3. Open the tier and add items. Each is one of:
    • Prize: a physical item, such as a patch or towel.
    • Cookie Credit: a dollar amount a family can put toward a destination.
    • Achievement: something earned by meeting a requirement (for example a booth charm). It's shown with the requirement and needs no choice.
  4. On an item you can turn on Scout chooses between this and other options. All choose-one items in a tier form one group, e.g. Beach Towel OR T-Shirt OR $10 Cookie Credits.
  5. If an item needs a choice, such as a size, turn on the attribute switch and pick Shirt sizes, Sock sizes, or build your own list under What to ask.

Items every scout gets in a tier appear under Every scout gets.

Import and export

Import / Export Rewards lets you work in a spreadsheet. Download a template with example rows, or export this season's rewards to edit or reuse next season. The preview shows what will be added or updated before anything saves, and importing never deletes.

The family selection window

Under Family Selection Window you set the Last day to choose and optionally the opening date. Families can choose rewards and send Cookie Credits only inside the window; managers can change anyone's choices at any time.

  • The window opens on the date you set, or 21 days before the Cookie Program Official End Date if you don't set one.
  • It closes after your troop deadline. Families are locked out afterward.

Cookie credits

Cookie Credits are fixed dollar amounts attached to reward tiers (either given outright or as one side of a choose-one). Under Cookie Credit Destinations, add the places families can send them, for example camp tuition, the council store, the troop activity fund, or registration. Each family then splits their scout's credits among the destinations. If council's report differs from the app's calculation, you can override a scout's credit total.

Track who still needs to choose

Scout Reward Status shows who still needs to choose or designate credits, flags picks that went stale after a return, and totals items by size and credits by destination: the numbers for your council order.

While the window is open, the dashboard shows a countdown card. For you it says how many scouts still need to choose; families see it for their own scouts. Reminders are in-app only.
