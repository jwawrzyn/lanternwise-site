Every time cookies or money change hands, you record a transfer. The app does the stock math for you.

How stock is counted

  • Cupboard (what the troop is holding): received from council + received from other troops + returns from scouts − given to scouts − given to other troops − boxes out at booths. It's calculated, never typed in. The app blocks a hand-off that would take the cupboard below zero.
  • Responsible For (per scout): what the scout's family took, minus returns and boxes given away or sent to a booth. It's what the family owes money on.
  • At Home (per scout): Responsible For minus boxes already delivered to customers.
  • Out at Booth: boxes at the scout's open personal booth.

The Inventory Dashboard shows the cupboard by cookie, plus a Cards view (one card per scout: money owed, a colour-coded bar, exact counts, and progress to the scout's box goal) and a Council table view (cookies across the top, scouts down the side). Money owed is the price of the boxes held minus the cash turned in and the card payments made online through Digital Cookie.

The dashboard also warns when a scout has promised more for delivery than they hold ("TM: promised 12, has 8 (short 4)").

Record a transfer

  1. Tap Transfer on the Inventory Dashboard (or open it from Transfer History & Receipts).
  2. Pick the route (below).
  3. Pick the scout if the route involves one.
  4. Enter Cases and Loose Boxes for each cookie, using +/− or tapping the number to type it. If money is changing hands, enter the Cash Amount ($) and how it was paid.
  5. Collect signatures (and the council receipt photo, if your Settings require it), then save.
RouteUse it when
Council ➔ TroopYour council delivery arrives. Do this first, so the cupboard has stock to give out. A photo of the paper council receipt can be attached.
Troop ➔ ScoutHanding cookies to a family.
Scout ➔ TroopA family returns cookies and/or turns in money.
Scout ➔ ScoutMoving boxes between scouts or families (see Let Families Give Cookies to Any Scout in Settings).
Troop ➔ Ext. Troop / Ext. Troop ➔ TroopSwapping with another troop. Enter their troop number and contact name, phone, and email; the receipt is emailed to that contact.
Troop ➔ Hometown HeroesGiving leftover cookies out to fulfill Hometown Heroes donations. It's a count; no signatures are needed.

Donation varieties (HTH, GOC) also appear on Troop ➔ Scout, Scout ➔ Troop, and Scout ➔ Scout, as boxes only. They only change a scout's donation total; they never touch the cupboard or a scout's stock or money. On a Troop ➔ Scout or Scout ➔ Troop transfer, each donation line has an Add to scout / Take from scout choice.

Receipts and history

Every transfer produces a receipt, emailed to the troop managers and the families involved (and the other troop's contact, for swaps). Open a transfer from Transfer History & Receipts to see it, add a missing receipt photo, or confirm a return a family recorded.

Confirm family returns. If you allow families to record transfers, a return doesn't count toward stock or what the family owes until a manager confirms it on the Transfers screen.

Donations screen

Donations (HTH & GOC) shows donation totals, how many Hometown Heroes boxes are sold, fulfilled, and still owed, and online donations. You can also move boxes from one donation type to another, troop-wide or for a single scout.
