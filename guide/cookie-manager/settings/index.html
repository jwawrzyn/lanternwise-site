Open Settings & Permissions (dashboard → Troop Management). Settings are grouped into five collapsible sections. Each section has its own Save button, which stays grey until you change something, so saving one section never overwrites another.

1. Booth Sign-Up Rules

Controls how many troop booth shifts each scout can take, and when. The goal is fairness: you release shifts in rounds so every family gets a chance at the good spots before anyone takes them all.

  • Add Round creates a round with an Opens date and time and a number of shifts. The first round is Shifts each scout can sign up for; later rounds are Additional shifts on top of the earlier ones (the screen shows the running total).
  • Open to Everyone (No Shift Limit) is the date after which the limit disappears. It must come after your last round.
  • With no rounds and no date, there's no limit and sign-ups are open immediately.

Example: Round 1 opens Friday for 2 shifts each, Round 2 opens the next Friday for 1 more (3 total), and everything opens to all the week after.

What the rounds don't cover: personal booths, and troop booths that have their own sign-up date set in the booth form, sit outside the rounds. A manager can still assign a scout over the limit after confirming the warning.

2. Parent Visibility & Personal Booths

SettingDefaultWhat it does
Allow Parents to View Troop CupboardOffOff: parents see only their own scout's cookies. On: families also get a read-only Troop Cupboard Totals card.
Allow Personal Driveway Booth RequestsOnLets families request their own storefront or driveway booths. Turn it off if your troop doesn't run them. See Personal booth requests.
Personal Booths Count as the Scout's Own SalesOffThe default sales mode for new personal booths. Off: the booth reconciles its own cash and credits the scout with what sells when it's finished. On: boxes the scout brings stay on what her family owes and what sells counts as her own sales, with no booth cash or credit split, and cupboard cookies go to the scout first. Applies to new booths; you can change an individual booth before it has stock.
Let Families Give Cookies to Any ScoutOffOn: families can record a Scout ➔ Scout transfer to any scout in the troop. Off: they can only move boxes between their own scouts, and you record transfers between families.
Let Families Record TransfersOnOn: families can record returns to the troop (with cash or checks) and Scout ➔ Scout transfers from their phone. A return doesn't count toward stock or what the family owes until you confirm it on the Transfers screen. Off: only managers record these.

3. Transfer Receipts

How cookie and money hand-offs are proven. Every transfer is still emailed to the troop managers and the families involved.

SettingDefaultWhat it does
In-App SignaturesOnThe giver and receiver both sign on the phone before a transfer saves.
Council Receipt PhotoOffTake a photo of the council's paper receipt. It's stored with the transfer and attached to the emailed receipt. A transfer saved without one is flagged so you can add the photo later. Council generally wants the paper receipt in a dispute, so many troops turn this on.
Receipt IDsOffAdds a switch to transfers that gives them the next receipt number, like T123-0001. It's handy for transfers that don't come from council, like Troop ➔ Scout. Numbers restart each season.
Receipt ID Prefix / Start Numbering Atblank / 1The letters before the number, and the first number to use. The screen shows an example. Because each phone reserves a batch of numbers while it has signal, IDs never repeat but can skip a few and may not be in exact time order.

If signatures and photos are both off, transfers are recorded without either.

4. Booth Reconciliation

How troop booths are wrapped up: cookies checked out and returned, cash counted, and the digital total. These apply to troop booths; personal booths follow the transfer signature setting above. All four are off by default, meaning only a manager reconciles troop booths and no one signs.

SettingWhat it does
Families Can Start ReconciliationThe family of a scout on a booth's shift can check cookies out and in and count the cash from their phone. They can't enter the digital total or finish the booth. You finalize it, and the booth can't be finished until you do.
Signature on CheckoutThe family signs when cookies are checked out to a troop booth. When you do the checkout, you both sign; when a family does it, you countersign when you finalize.
Signature on ReturnThe same, for unsold cookies coming back.
Signature on CashThe family signs for the cash they hand in; you sign when you finalize.

Credit cards are processed through Digital Cookie, so credit card and digital are a single amount that you enter when you finalize.
