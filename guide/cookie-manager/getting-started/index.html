This page takes you from nothing to a working troop. You only do it once: the first Troop Cookie Manager (TCM) creates the troop, and everyone else joins it by invite.

Before you start

  • Install the Cookie Manager app on your phone.
  • Have your troop number handy.
  • Use an email address you can open on your phone. You'll need to tap a confirmation link.

Create your account and start the troop

  1. Open the app and tap Sign Up. Enter your email and a password.
  2. We email you a confirmation link. Tap it. The page says Email Confirmed. If you don't see the email, check spam, then use Send It Again in the app.
  3. Back in the app, tap I've Confirmed It. The Complete Your Profile screen opens.
  4. Enter your full name.
  5. Choose Start a New Troop, enter your troop number, and tap Create Troop.

You're now the troop's owner and a Troop Cookie Manager, and you land on the dashboard.

Only start a troop if it isn't in the app yet. If another manager already set up your troop, don't create a second one. Ask them for an invite (see Managers, scouts and families) and use Join with a Code instead.

If you were invited instead

Open the invite link on your phone. It takes you to sign-up with your troop already filled in. Create your account, confirm your email, and the join code is entered for you. Tap Join Troop. A manager invite makes you a co-manager, and a family code makes you a parent.

Your dashboard

The dashboard is the home screen. As a manager you'll see these sections:

Cards with an orange border and a number need your attention, for example family cookie requests waiting for a decision or booths that need wrapping up.

If you're also a parent with a scout in the troop, you'll see a My Family card too.

Suggested order for setting up a season

  1. Create the season and add your cookies.
  2. Invite co-managers, add scouts, and invite families.
  3. Review Settings so the app matches how your troop works.
  4. Enter the reward tiers from your council's materials.
  5. Set your cupboard address and hours if families will pick up cookies from you.
  6. Schedule booths.
  7. Record the council delivery when your cookies arrive.

Working offline

Every screen opens offline with the last data it saw, and the most common jobs (transfers with signatures, booth stock and cash, deliveries, new customers and orders) save on your phone and sync when you have signal. A banner shows sync status. A few things need a connection: booth scheduling and sign-ups, voiding transfers, and the manager screens that change settings.
