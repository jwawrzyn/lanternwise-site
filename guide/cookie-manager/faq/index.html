Common questions

A parent can't see their scout.

The parent must have a confirmed email, and their email must be on the scout or linked by hand from the roster. Ask them to tap the link we emailed (the dashboard has a Confirm Your Email card with a resend option), then check the scout shows Linked on the roster.

The cupboard number looks wrong.

The cupboard is calculated from transfers, so look for a missing one: the council delivery recorded as Council ➔ Troop, returns recorded as Scout ➔ Troop, and booth checkouts and returns. A negative number means more was handed out than was received, often because the council delivery hasn't been recorded yet.

A family says they returned cookies but nothing changed.

If families record their own returns, you need to confirm the return on the Transfers screen before it counts toward stock or what they owe.

I made a mistake on a transfer, order, or booth.

Customer orders can be edited from the delivery list. Booths can be reopened (Reopen Booth, Reopen Reconciliation). Transfers can be voided when you're online. If you're stuck, send us a note (below).

Can I use it with no signal?

Yes, for the day-to-day work. Transfers, booth stock and cash, deliveries, new customers, and orders save on your phone and sync when you're back online. Scheduling booths, sign-ups, and changing settings need a connection.

How do I remove a co-manager?

Only the troop owner can. See Managers, scouts and families.

Something looks broken.

Open the Diagnostics screen (link at the bottom of the dashboard) and tap Send to App Team to send us the app's log along with a note. Or email support@lanternwise.app. To delete your account see Delete account.

Glossary

TermMeaning
TCMTroop Cookie Manager. A volunteer with manager access to the whole troop's cookie data.
OwnerThe TCM who created the troop. Only the owner can remove other managers or hand over ownership.
Family / ParentA parent or guardian login. It can only see the scouts linked to it.
CupboardThe cookies the troop holds in storage, before they go to families. It's calculated from transfers.
Responsible ForBoxes a scout's family took and hasn't returned. The basis of what they owe.
At HomeResponsible For minus what's been delivered to customers.
TransferA signed record of cookies and/or money changing hands.
Troop boothA booth the troop schedules and staffs with shifts.
Personal boothA booth a family runs on its own. It needs council approval.
ShiftA time slot at a troop booth that scouts sign up for.
RoundA step in the rolling release of booth shifts. Each round raises the number of shifts a scout may take.
ReconciliationCounting what a booth sold: stock checked out minus returned, against cash and digital money.
FinalizeThe TCM's final sign-off on a reconciliation, entering the digital total.
Digital CookieThe Girl Scouts' online ordering platform. It handles card payments.
HTHHometown Heroes: a local donation the troop fulfills from leftover cookies.
GOCGift of Caring: a virtual donation that council fulfills.
Cookie CreditsDollar amounts a scout earns at reward tiers, which the family sends to a destination such as camp tuition.
TierA box-total milestone with rewards. A scout earns every tier at or below her total.
Selection windowThe period when families can choose rewards and designate credits.
