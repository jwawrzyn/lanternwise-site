What is a "New Customer Order"?

A customer order is a record of cookies a scout has sold directly to a customer: a neighbour, a relative, a coworker. It records who bought, which cookies and how many, whether they've paid, and whether the cookies have been delivered yet. It's separate from booth sales and from Digital Cookie online orders.

The New Customer Order button on the dashboard is for families to log sales (and for you to log sales for your own scout, or to fix an order). As a manager you'll mostly see these orders through the reports and the delivery stock checks.

Entering an order

  1. Select Scout (families pick from their own scouts).
  2. Select or Add Customer. A customer has a name (required), phone, email, delivery address, and porch or drop-off notes. Edit Details on a customer changes it on all of their orders.
  3. Order Line Items: cases and boxes per cookie. Donation boxes (HTH, GOC) can be on an order too; there's nothing to deliver for those.
  4. Payment Details: Payment Status is Unpaid, Partially Paid, or Paid. Payment Method is Cash, Check (with a check number), or Electronic (Venmo, CashApp, Zelle, Square, Digital Cookie).
  5. Add order or delivery notes and save. Orders can be saved offline.

Made a mistake? Open the order from the delivery list and tap Edit. Editing needs a connection.

Cash from customers. A customer's cash is kept by the family. It reaches you when the family turns money in on a Scout ➔ Troop transfer, and the scout's balance drops then.

Delivery Route Pipeline

Delivery Route Pipeline lists orders by Needs Delivery and Delivered. Each pending order shows an In Stock badge, or Short by N boxes if the scout doesn't have enough at home. Map Delivery Route in Google Maps builds a route through the addresses, and marking an order delivered reduces the scout's At Home count. Deliveries stay per scout: a sibling's boxes must be moved with a Scout ➔ Scout transfer first.

Digital Cookie import

Digital Cookie is the Girl Scouts' online ordering platform. It collects card payments itself, and you bring those orders into the app with its CSV export so stock, money owed, and reward totals stay right.

  1. On the dashboard tap Digital Cookie Import.
  2. Pick the CSV you exported from Digital Cookie.
  3. Check the Match this file panel. It opens by itself only when something needs your choice. Headings and wording change from year to year, so you tell the app which column is the order number, scout, date, type, payment, amount, and status; which catalog cookie each cookie column is; and what each order type, payment, and status in the file means. The choices are saved for your troop and applied to the next file.
  4. Review the preview. Every order is checked; nothing is saved until you confirm.
  5. Fix any unmatched scout names (pick them by hand or leave them out), then confirm.

DonationThe boxes go to the scout's donation totals, under the donation cookie you pick.
  • Safe to repeat. It keeps one row per Digital Cookie order number per season, so re-importing the same or a longer file only adds new orders.
  • Cancelled, rejected, and refunded orders are skipped. Partly refunded orders and total mismatches get a warning.
  • Troop-site orders (no scout named) usually came from a booth, so you assign them to a booth (or a scout). The app groups them by time and, when the file names the booth, by that name, pre-picks the matching booth, and lets you change any group. They don't touch any scout's stock, credit, or rewards, because the booth's own stock already covers them.
  • Orders typed in by hand that match a file order (same scout, same customer name) are offered for merging. The Digital Cookie data wins. You can also tap Merge with Digital Cookie on an order later.
  • Moving orders afterwards. The Imported orders list groups everything by who has it. Tick orders and move them to a scout, a booth, or back to unassigned.
