Open Reports from the dashboard (Season Management). Each report has a Share as CSV button that opens the phone's share sheet; the file opens in Excel or Sheets. Reports work offline with the last data they saw.

The reports

ReportWhat it shows
Inventory reconciliationFor each cookie: what council gave you and what the cupboard should hold, beside boxes with scouts, boxes still at home, at or sold at booths, promised for delivery, and open requests and returns. An After column shows the cupboard once open requests are carried out and warns if it can't cover them.
Bank deposit ledgerMoney collected (scout cash, checks, and electronic turn-ins, plus cash from finished booths) against money deposited, with a running pending balance.
Financial summarySeason sales by channel (customer orders, Digital Cookie, booths), how money came in by payment method, and what each scout still owes.
Sales leaderboard & booth metricsBoxes per scout (same total as rewards; ties share a rank) and boxes per hour at finished booths by location and by shift type.
Council exportOne row per scout with everything council needs: boxes (taken, booth, donations, shipped, adjustments), tiers earned, reward items with sizes, credits earned and where they were designated, credits not yet designated, and what the family still owes. Scouts with no boxes are left out.

Log a bank deposit

  1. Tap Log a Bank Deposit on the dashboard (or open the deposit ledger).
  2. Enter the date, the amount, a slip or check number, and any notes.
  3. Save. You can edit or delete a deposit later (this needs a connection).

The ledger tracks the pending balance: cash and checks you're holding that haven't been deposited. Cash from booths that aren't finished yet shows as not counted yet. Card and other digital money belongs to Digital Cookie and isn't part of a deposit.

Before you send the council export

The screen lists scouts whose rewards or credits are incomplete and totals prizes by size, so you can chase anyone missing before sharing.
