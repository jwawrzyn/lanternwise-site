Getting and returning cookies

Request more cookies, book a cupboard time, return extras, and give boxes to another scout.

The troop keeps cookies in a cupboard and hands them out as families need them. You ask for more, or bring extras back, from each scout's card under My Family. Your troop's cupboard address shows on your home screen with an Open in Maps button.

Ask for more cookies

  1. On your scout's card tap Request or Return Cookies.
  2. On the Request Cookies tab, pick the scout if you have several.
  3. Enter cases and boxes for each cookie. We start with whatever your scout is short on. Change it however you like. Use + and − or tap the number to type.
  4. Add an optional note, then tap Send Request.

Your request starts as Requested. The cookie manager Approves or Rejects it, and it becomes Fulfilled once the cookies change hands. You can see all your requests and their status on the same screen, and cancel one while it's Requested or Approved.

Return cookies you can't sell

Use the Return Cookies tab the same way. A return can't be more than your scout has at home.

If your troop allows it, you'll also see Return to Troop on the scout's card. This records a return, plus any cash or checks you're turning in, straight from your phone. It doesn't count until your cookie manager confirms it, so your balance won't change right away.

Book a cupboard time

If your troop uses cupboard hours, you pick a 15-minute time when you send a request or return. Only one family can have each time.

  • One visit can cover several requests and returns, for example a swap, or two scouts.
  • You can add to a visit you already booked.
  • Tap Change Time to move it, or Cancel Request / Cancel Return to cancel.
  • If no times are posted yet, you can still send the request and the cookie manager will contact you.
  • Your troop sets a cutoff: past it you can't book or cancel on your own, so contact your cookie manager.
  • If a visit is cancelled or missed, your request stays open and shows Pick a Time so you can book another.

Give boxes to another scout

If you have several scouts, tap Give to a Sibling to move boxes between them. Your troop may also let you use Give to a Scout to move boxes to any scout in the troop. Otherwise, ask your cookie manager to record a transfer to another family.

Receipts and history

When cookies or money change hands, both sides sign in the app if your troop uses signatures, and a receipt is emailed. Past transfers are under Transfer History & Receipts.
