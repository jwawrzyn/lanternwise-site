Tap My Family on the dashboard to see each of your scouts' cookies. If you have more than one scout, a Family Total card adds them together first.

The three numbers

NumberWhat it means
Responsible ForBoxes your family has taken from the troop and not given back. This is what you owe money on, whether or not the boxes have been sold yet.
At HomeResponsible For minus what you've already delivered to customers: the boxes you should physically have.
Out at BoothBoxes at your scout's open personal booth.

A table lists every cookie with Home, Owed (Responsible For) and, when relevant, Booth columns. A count below zero usually means a transfer is missing: ask your cookie manager to check it.

Money Summary

Tap Money Summary to see, per scout and for the family:

  • cookies received,
  • cash and checks you've turned in,
  • anything customers paid online through Digital Cookie (shown as paid online),
  • the balance: what your family owes the troop, which is the price of the cookies you've received minus what you've turned in and what was paid online,
  • what your customers have paid you, and what they still owe you on your own orders.
Cash you collect from customers stays with you until you turn it in to the troop. Your balance doesn't drop until the cookie manager records it.

Short on cookies?

If you've promised more cookies to customers than you have at home, your scout's card shows a warning like "TM: promised 12, has 8 (short 4)". Use Request or Return Cookies to ask for more. See Getting and returning cookies.

Booth credits

Tap View Booth Shifts & Sales Credits to see the booth shifts your scout worked and the itemized cookie credits she earned from them.
