This guide is for parents and guardians using the Cookie Manager app during cookie season. Your troop's cookie manager sets everything up; you join, and then you can see your scout's cookies, take orders, sign up for booths, and choose rewards.

Join your troop

Your troop cookie manager will send you an invite link or a join code (it looks like 12345-KQMXRT).

With an invite link

  1. Install the Cookie Manager app, then tap the link on your phone. It opens sign-up with your troop already filled in.
  2. Enter your email and a password and tap Sign Up.
  3. We email you a confirmation link. Tap it. If you can't find it, check spam, or tap Send It Again in the app.
  4. Back in the app, tap I've Confirmed It, enter your full name, and tap Join Troop.

With a code

Sign up and confirm your email the same way. On Complete Your Profile, choose Join with a Code, type the code, and tap Join Troop.

Don't tap "Start a New Troop". That's only for a cookie manager whose troop isn't in the app yet.

Get your scout showing up

Joining puts you in the troop, but you only see your own scouts once the cookie manager has linked them to you. That happens automatically when:

  • the troop has your email address on your scout, and
  • you've confirmed that email address.

Use the same email you gave the troop. If your scout still doesn't appear, the home screen says No scouts are currently linked to your account. Ask your cookie manager to link you using your account email. Everything else on this page works once the link is made.

More than one parent? Each parent or guardian can have their own login linked to the same scout. Ask the cookie manager to add each email.

The home screen

After signing in you land on the troop dashboard. Look for these:

  • My Family: your scouts' cookies, what you owe, and booth credits.
  • Customer Sales & Deliveries: New Customer Order and Delivery Route Pipeline.
  • Booths: Booth Schedule & Operations, where you sign up for shifts and request personal booths.
  • The rewards card, when it's time to choose.
  • Transfer History & Receipts: past hand-offs of cookies and money.
  • A Troop Cupboard Totals card, if your troop turns that on.

Cards with an orange border and a number need your attention.

Using the app with no signal

Screens you've opened before still show the last data they saw, and customer orders, deliveries and transfers save on your phone and sync when you're back online. Booth sign-ups and booth requests need a connection.
