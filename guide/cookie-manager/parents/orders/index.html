What is a customer order?

A customer order is a record of cookies your scout has sold directly to someone: a neighbour, a relative, a coworker. It tracks who bought, which cookies, whether they've paid, and whether you've delivered yet. It's separate from booth sales and from Digital Cookie online orders.

Add an order

  1. Tap New Customer Order on the dashboard.
  2. Select Scout if you have more than one.
  3. Select or Add Customer. A new customer needs a name; phone, email, delivery address and porch notes are optional but make deliveries easier. Next time, pick the customer from the list.
  4. Order Line Items: cases and boxes per cookie. Donation boxes (Hometown Heroes, Gift of Caring) can go on an order too, and there's nothing to deliver for them.
  5. Payment Details: choose Unpaid, Partially Paid or Paid, and the method: cash, check (with check number), or electronic (Venmo, CashApp, Zelle, Square, Digital Cookie).
  6. Add any order or delivery notes and save. Orders save offline and sync later.

To fix a mistake, open the order from the delivery list and tap Edit. Use Edit Details on a customer to fix their name, phone or address on all of their orders. Editing needs a connection.

Deliver

Delivery Route Pipeline lists orders by Needs Delivery and Delivered.

  • Each order shows In Stock if you have the cookies at home, or Short by N boxes if you don't. Request more from the troop before you head out.
  • Map Delivery Route in Google Maps sets up a route through your customers' addresses.
  • When you've handed the cookies over, mark the order delivered. Your At Home count drops to match.
Siblings: deliveries are per scout. If one scout is short, move boxes from a sibling first with Give to a Sibling.

Digital Cookie orders

Orders customers place online through Digital Cookie are brought into the app by your cookie manager. You don't enter them. Card payments made online count toward your balance as paid online.
