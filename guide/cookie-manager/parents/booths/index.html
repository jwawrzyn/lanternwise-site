Open Booth Schedule & Operations from the dashboard. There are two kinds of booth.

KindWhat it is
Troop BoothScheduled by the cookie manager at a store or location. Scouts sign up for shifts.
Personal StandA booth your family arranges, such as a driveway or storefront. It needs council approval.

Sign up for a troop booth shift

  1. Find a booth. The filters help: Upcoming, Past, All, Open spots for shifts that still need people, and My scouts for yours.
  2. On a shift with room, tap Assign Scout, choose your scout, and confirm.
  • The list updates live as other families sign up. If someone takes the last spot first, you'll see Spot Just Taken and the shift shows Full.
  • Your troop may release shifts in rounds: each round lets every scout sign up for more shifts. A banner shows your limit. If sign-ups aren't open yet, you'll see Sign-Ups Locked.
  • You can remove your own scout from a shift. If you can't make it, do that early so someone else can take the spot.
  • On any upcoming booth, Add to Calendar opens your phone's new-event screen with the time and scouts filled in, and Directions opens the address in Maps.

Request a personal booth

If your troop allows personal booths, tap Request a Personal Booth.

  1. Choose the scout, date, start time and duration.
  2. Enter the location name and the street address (required).
  3. Enter the site contact: the first name, last name, email and phone of the person at the location who agreed to the booth. Council asks for this so they can confirm it.
  4. Save the request.

Your cookie manager sends the request to council, and you get an email at each step: Requested, Sent to Council, then Council Approved or Not Approved (with a reason, so you can ask again). Changing the scout, place or time later asks for approval again. Until a booth is approved, it's flagged as unapproved, but you can still run it.

Sales mode

Each personal booth is one of two kinds:

  • Booth sales: the booth settles its own cash. When you finish the booth, everything sold is credited to your scout.
  • Scout's own sales: the boxes come from your scout's own stock and stay on what your family owes. There's no separate booth cash or credit.

Your troop picks the default. Ask your cookie manager if you want a different one for a single booth.

Run your personal booth

  1. Open the booth and tap Check Out Cookies. Choose cookies from the troop cupboard (signed) or your scout's own boxes.
  2. Sell. You can use Open Sales Tracker to tap cookies as you sell, then Cash or Digital.
  3. At the end, tap Return Unsold (or Return all unsold) to return what's left.
  4. Record cash, digital sales and any donation boxes, then tap Finish Booth.

Anything not returned counts as sold. After a booth is over, the dashboard shows a count of personal booths to wrap up: return unsold boxes & finish until you do.

Troop booth wrap-up

For troop booths the cookie manager does the reconciliation. If your troop allows it, the family of a scout on the shift may be asked to check cookies out and back in, count the cash and tap Submit Reconciliation. The cookie manager then adds the digital total and finalizes. You may be asked to sign for checkout, return or cash.
