I joined, but I don't see my scout.

Your cookie manager needs to link your account to your scout. It happens automatically if your email is on your scout and you've confirmed it. Check the confirmation email, then ask your cookie manager to confirm which email they have.

I didn't get the confirmation email.

Check spam. Then tap Send It Again on the profile screen, or on the Confirm Your Email card on the dashboard.

Why doesn't my balance change after I returned cookies?

Returns and money you turn in only count after your cookie manager confirms them.

Why can't I book or cancel a cupboard time?

Your troop sets a cutoff, such as 24 hours ahead. After that, contact your cookie manager, who can always change a booking.

The numbers show a count below zero.

Usually a transfer wasn't recorded. Tell your cookie manager and they'll fix it.

I can't give cookies to another family's scout.

Your troop may only let you move boxes between your own scouts. Ask the cookie manager to record the transfer.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes for orders, deliveries and transfers, which sync later. Booth sign-ups and requests need a connection.

Something looks wrong.

Tell your cookie manager first. If it's an app problem, open Diagnostics (the link at the bottom of the dashboard) and tap Send to App Team, or email support@lanternwise.app. To delete your account, see Delete account.
