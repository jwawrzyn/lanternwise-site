Tap the rewards card on the dashboard, or Rewards & Cookie Credits under My Family, to see how your scout is doing.

How your scout's total is counted

Her total toward rewards is the boxes she's taken from the troop (whether or not they've been delivered yet), plus booth sales credits and donation boxes, plus any adjustment your cookie manager adds, such as Digital Cookie boxes shipped straight to customers. Tiers add up: she earns every tier at or below her total.

Set a personal goal

At the top of the rewards screen tap Set Goal (or Edit), enter how many boxes she wants to sell this season, and tap Save Goal. A progress bar shows how close she is. Leave it blank to clear it. You can change it any time.

Reward tiers

Under Reward Tiers you see each milestone, what's included, and what she's earned. Some tiers are marked BONUS: late-season goals worth aiming for.

  • Every scout gets items come automatically.
  • Choose one: some tiers let you pick between options, for example a beach towel or a T-shirt or $10 in Cookie Credits. Tap the one you want.
  • Sizes: where an item comes in sizes, pick one. A Still needed line lists what you haven't chosen yet.
  • Achievements (like a booth charm) show the requirement but need no choice.

If you have several scouts, switch between them with the scout buttons at the top. A number next to a name is how many choices are still outstanding.

Cookie Credits

Some tiers give Cookie Credits, a dollar amount to put toward something. The Cookie Credits section shows how much she's earned, how much you've designated and how much is left. For each destination your troop offers (for example camp tuition, the council store, troop activities or registration), enter a dollar amount, then tap Save Credit Choices.

Credit totals are set by your cookie manager from the council report, so they may be adjusted near the end.

The selection window

You can only choose within the window. It opens a few weeks before the end of the cookie program and closes at your troop's deadline. The dashboard card shows when choices open and the due date, and turns orange when a scout still needs to choose. After the deadline you're locked out, so ask your cookie manager if you miss it, because they can still change choices.

If a scout returns cookies after choosing, her total can drop below a tier. Check your choices once things settle.
