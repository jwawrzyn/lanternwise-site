Everything on this page happens on the Troop Roster screen (dashboard → Troop Management → Troop Roster).

Invite a co-manager

Troops can have several Troop Cookie Managers. Any manager can invite another.

  1. Open Troop Roster.
  2. Under Invite a Co-Manager, type their email address and tap Send Invite.
  3. We email them an invite. If the email can't be sent, the app tells you and you can share the link yourself with Text Link.
  4. The invite shows under Waiting to Join with its expiry date. Tap Cancel to withdraw it.
Good to know: an invite works once, only for that email address, and expires after 7 days. They sign up (or log in) with that same email.

Removing a manager and handing over ownership

  • The troop owner (the person who created the troop) is the only one who can Remove a co-manager. The removed person loses all access to troop data. Their past transfers and signatures stay on record, and they can't rejoin unless invited again.
  • The owner can tap Make Owner next to another manager to hand ownership over. You stay a manager, but only the new owner can remove managers or pass ownership on.

Add scouts

Add scouts one at a time, or import your whole roster.

One at a time

  1. Tap Add Scout.
  2. Enter the first and last name. The parent's email is optional.
  3. Tap Save Scout.

If you enter a parent's email, the scout links to that parent automatically, either right away (if they already have an account) or when they sign up and confirm that email. Tap Edit on a scout's card to fix a misspelling.

Import a roster from a spreadsheet

At the bottom of the roster is a collapsible import section.

  1. Download the CSV template. It has one row per scout, with the name and the emails of up to four parents or guardians.
  2. Fill it in with Excel or Sheets and save it as CSV.
  3. Pick the file in the app. Every row is checked and previewed before anything is saved.
  4. Confirm the import.

Importing is safe to repeat: scouts already on the roster (same name) just gain any new guardians, nothing is deleted, and importing the same file again changes nothing.

Bring families in

Families get their own logins. Each scout can have several parents or guardians, and a family with several scouts sees them all under My Family.

Share the family join code

At the top of the roster is the Family Join Code (it looks like 12345-KQMXRT). Tap Share Link to text or email an invite link. Families open it, sign up, confirm their email, and land in your troop. They can also type the code in under Join with a Code.

Joining with the code makes someone a parent in the troop. They only see their own scouts once you've linked them.

Link a parent to a scout

  • Automatic: put their email on the scout (when adding, or in the roster import). The link happens when that email has a confirmed account.
  • By hand: on the scout's card tap Link Parent, enter the parent's exact registered email, and tap Link Account. If no account exists for that email yet, the app says so.
  • Unlink: tap Remove next to a linked parent.

Each scout shows Linked or Unlinked and lists the guardians waiting on an account. Receipts and booth updates go to every guardian listed for the scout.

Reset the code if it leaks. Reset code makes the old join code stop working immediately. People who already joined stay in the troop; you share the new code with anyone who hasn't signed up yet.

What families can do

Families can see their scouts' cookies at home and what they owe, ask for or return cookies, sign up for booth shifts, request personal booths, record deliveries and customer orders, and choose rewards. What they can see and do beyond that is controlled by Settings.
