Everything here is under Cookie Catalog & Seasons (dashboard → Season Management).

Create a season

  1. Tap New Season.
  2. Enter a Season Title (e.g. 2026-2027 Cookie Season) and a Season Label (e.g. 2026-2027).
  3. Fill in the milestone dates, using the format MM/DD/YYYY (see below).
  4. Tap Save Season.

The active season is the one everything else in the app works from: orders, transfers, booths, rewards, and reports. Edit Season changes it later, and History lists past seasons. Receipt IDs restart each season.

The season dates

These come from your council's calendar. Fill in the ones you have; you can come back and add more.

Add your cookies

With a season selected, tap Add Cookie. For each variety you enter:

FieldNotes
Variety NameFor example Thin Mints.
Short Name (optional)A short label such as TM, used as a column heading in wide tables, like the council's own software. Leave it blank and the app makes one from the name.
TypeCookie, or one of two donation types (see below).
Box Price ($)The price of one box, like 6.00.
Case SizeBoxes per case, like 12. This is how the app converts cases to boxes everywhere.
StatusActive or Archived. Archive a variety you no longer sell instead of deleting it, so past records stay intact.

Tap a cookie to edit it.

Match your council receipt order

Tap Reorder, arrange the cookies in the same order as your council receipt, and tap Save Order. Every cookie list, entry form, and emailed receipt uses that order, which makes checking a delivery against the paper receipt much easier.

Donation varieties: Hometown Heroes and Gift of Caring

Donations are added to the catalog like cookies but with a donation Type:

  • Local donation (troop gives leftover cookies), for example Hometown Heroes. Customers pay for boxes that the troop later fulfills from leftover cookies.
  • Virtual donation (council fulfills), for example Gift of Caring.

Donation boxes are sold one box at a time and are never part of the cupboard or a scout's boxes. They count toward a scout's totals and can have their own reward tiers. Use the Donations (HTH & GOC) screen to see how many Hometown Heroes boxes are sold, fulfilled, and still owed. The type can't be changed after the variety is saved.
