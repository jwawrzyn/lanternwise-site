Personal booth requests

How families request a personal booth, and how you take each request to council and back.

A personal booth is a stand a family runs on its own, such as in a driveway or outside a shop. Council wants to approve each one, so every personal booth goes through a queue you manage.

What families do

  1. On the booth screen, tap Request a Personal Booth.
  2. Choose the scout, date, start time, and duration. Enter the location name and a required street address.
  3. Enter the site contact: the first name, last name, email, and phone of the person at the site who OK'd the booth, so council can confirm it. The form checks the details before saving.
  4. The request goes to you as Requested.

Turn off Allow Personal Driveway Booth Requests in Settings if your troop doesn't use them. You can also schedule a personal booth for a family yourself.

What you do with each request

Open Personal Booth Requests from the dashboard. The count badge shows what's waiting. The queue has three tabs:

TabWhat's in it
Needs ReviewNew requests. Each shows the scout, place, address, time, notes, and the site contact (tap to call or email).
With CouncilRequests you've sent and are waiting on.
DecidedApproved and not-approved booths.
  1. Review the request, then tap Sent to Council after you've submitted it through council's own process. The app doesn't contact council; it tracks where each request stands.
  2. When council answers, tap Approved (you can add an optional note for the family) or Not Approved (a reason is required, and it's sent to the family so they can try again).
  3. Made a mistake? Use Back to Requested.

You get an email for each new request, and the family gets an email at every step.

Good to know

  • Unapproved booths still work. A booth waiting on council, or not approved, can still be operated, but it's flagged as unapproved.
  • Changing the scout, place, or time asks for approval again. A request already sent, approved, or denied goes back to Requested. Editing only the site contact doesn't.
  • TCM-created personal booths also start as Requested. Troop booths are always approved.
  • Sales mode. Each personal booth is either Booth sales (the booth reconciles its own cash; what sells is credited to the scout when it's finished) or Scout's own sales (her boxes stay on what her family owes; no booth cash or credit split). The troop default comes from Settings, and you can change it for one booth before it has any stock.
  • Families run it. The family checks out cookies from the cupboard (signed) or uses the scout's own boxes, then returns unsold boxes and finishes the booth. Return all unsold speeds that up.
