Scheduling and running booths

Schedule troop booths, control sign-ups, and reconcile and finish each booth.

Open Booth Schedule & Operations from the dashboard. As a manager you operate troop booths. Families run their own personal booths.

Schedule a troop booth

  1. Tap Schedule First Booth Slot (once you have booths, use the add button on the booth list).
  2. Booth Type: choose Troop Booth.
  3. Pick the Booth Date and Start Time.
  4. Choose the Shift Duration in hours and the number of Scouts per Booth. That number is a firm limit for everyone, managers included.
  5. Enter the Location Name (e.g. Walmart North) and the street address. Address and notes help families find it.
  6. Optional: set sign-up window overrides (below), then save.

Tap a booth later to edit it. If you've already got a list, use import.

Import many booths at once

The booth import is on the booth list screen. Download the CSV template, fill it in a spreadsheet (date, start time, location, and either an end time or a duration are required), and pick the file. Every row is checked and previewed first, duplicates are skipped, and rows with errors are listed so the good ones can still be imported.

Sign-up window overrides

By default a booth follows the troop-wide round rules. In the booth form you can override that for one booth:

  • Sign-Ups Open / Close Date/Time: give this booth its own window. A booth with its own open date doesn't count toward the round limit.
  • Default (Date Driven), Force Open, Force Closed: follow the dates, open it right now, or lock sign-ups.

Assign scouts to shifts

Each shift shows who's working it and how many spots remain. Tap Assign Scout on a shift and search for the scout by name. If a scout is over the round limit you're warned and can choose Assign Anyway. You can remove a worker at any time.

Families sign up on their own phones. The list updates live as other families sign up, and the database stops two families taking the last spot: the second sees Spot Just Taken.

The booth list has filters for Upcoming (the default), Past and All, plus Open spots to find shifts that still need people. Each booth card has Add to Calendar and Directions buttons for families and for you.

Run a booth: reconcile and finish

Open the booth. The steps, in order:

  1. Check out cookies. Tap Check Out Cookies and enter boxes and cases per variety. This is a transfer from the cupboard to the booth, signed if your settings require it.
  2. Track sales (optional). Open Sales Tracker: tap cookies to build an order, then tap Cash or Digital. There's an 8-second undo, any order can be voided later, and several phones can ring up sales at once, even offline. The tracker is a check on stock, not a replacement. If the tally and stock disagree, the booth screen flags each variety, and Use Tally for Cash Register fills in the cash, digital, and donation fields.
  3. Return unsold. Tap Return Unsold and enter what came back. Boxes sold = checked out − returned.
  4. Record donations. Donation boxes customers paid for (Hometown Heroes or Gift of Caring). Nothing leaves the booth. If the cash is over, the app suggests recording the overage as Gift of Caring.
  5. Count the cash. Enter Starting Cash Float, Cash Collected, and Digital / Credit Card. The screen compares expected sales revenue with the payments you logged and shows the Cash Variance. Tap Save Cash Register Logs.
  6. Assign credits. Assign Credits by Variety gives shift workers whole-box credit for sales (these are what count for reward totals). Credits can be split equally, or by variety.
  7. Finish. Tap Finish Booth. Anything not returned counts as sold. Reopen Booth undoes it.
Booth money is settled at the booth. Scouts on a shift get box credits only. The app doesn't split dollars or make a scout owe booth proceeds.

If you let families start reconciliation

With Families Can Start Reconciliation turned on in Settings, the family of a scout on the shift can check out and return cookies, enter donations, count the cash, and tap Submit Reconciliation. You then enter the digital total, review the variance, and tap Finalize Reconciliation. If signatures are on, you countersign once for checkout, return, and cash. Until you finalize, the booth can't be finished. Reopen Reconciliation undoes it. After finalizing, families can no longer change the booth's stock, cash, or donations.

Online orders at a booth

If you've imported Digital Cookie orders for this booth (see Orders and Digital Cookie), a Digital Cookie Orders card shows the orders, boxes, card, and cash, with a button to use the card total as the booth's digital amount.

Booths that need wrapping up

A booth that's over but not finished gets flagged: a count on the dashboard, a banner and filter on the booth list, and a flag on the booth itself. Managers see troop and family booths separately. Finish or reopen them to clear the flag.
